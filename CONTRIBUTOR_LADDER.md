# Contributor Ladder

This ladder describes contribution responsibilities, not social rank. Advancement is based on demonstrated work, fairness, and trust; no level grants authority over participants' rights.

| Level | Typical work | Evidence to progress | Authority boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Observer | Read docs, report unclear text or reproducible issues | A clear issue or useful documentation correction | No privileged access |
| 2. Contributor | Submit focused docs, tests, fixes or translations | Reviewed changes; tests and rationale included | No merge authority |
| 3. Regular contributor | Own a small subsystem or compatibility test suite | Several quality changes, review responsiveness, secure practices | No unilateral release or policy authority |
| 4. Maintainer candidate | Review changes, manage triage, help releases | Documented peer feedback, security awareness, conflict-of-interest disclosure | Temporary/revocable delegation; least privilege |
| 5. Maintainer | Maintain a subsystem and participate in review | Public appointment process, demonstrated continuity and handover | Cannot self-approve high-risk changes affecting their own privileges |
| 6. Governance/oversight role | Participate in governance or independent review | Separate election/appointment process and disclosed interests | Must not combine oversight with sole control of the audited process |

## Review and safeguards

- Contributors can raise concerns without retaliation.
- Decisions and material conflicts of interest are recorded publicly, except genuinely sensitive security details.
- Maintainer rights are granted with minimum permissions and periodically reviewed.
- A maintainer may be removed only through a documented process with notice, reasons, a chance to respond, and appeal.
- No contribution volume, sponsorship, employment, or founder relationship automatically confers governance control.
- Security reports follow `SECURITY.md`; do not require public disclosure of exploitable details.
- Every release needs at least one reviewer who is not the primary author of the release-critical changes.
