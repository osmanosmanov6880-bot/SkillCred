# Pilot playbook: international / cross-border project

## Goal
Test transfer and verification across organizations, languages, software stacks and jurisdictions without assuming that one issuer or legal regime is universally trusted.

## Workflow
1. Agree credential vocabulary, dates, locale rules and supported cryptosuite in writing.
2. Exchange synthetic credentials between at least two independently operated implementations.
3. Publish a trust-policy statement identifying who is authorized to issue which achievements and how that authority is reviewed.
4. Test translations, right-to-left rendering in downstream wallet experiences and data-minimization choices.
5. Record which claims are machine-verified and which require human/legal review.

## Stop conditions
Cross-border data transfers lack a lawful basis, a relying party treats issuer recognition as automatic, or a credential is silently normalized into a different achievement claim.
