# Pilot playbook: employer / skills-based hiring

## Goal
Test whether hiring teams can validate a narrowly defined practical skill more quickly while preserving applicant choice and alternative evidence paths.

## Workflow
1. Choose a non-regulated skill and define a practical assessment rubric.
2. Do not require SkillCred participation as a condition of applying.
3. Ask candidates to share a credential voluntarily and retain the option to demonstrate the same skill another way.
4. Compare verification time against the employer's existing process using an agreed baseline.
5. Separate credential authenticity from skill quality and issuer reputation; reviewers must assess each separately.
6. Delete exported pilot records at the agreed retention date.

## Stop conditions
Credentials are treated as an automatic hiring decision, unverified issuer prestige becomes a proxy for candidate worth, or records are reused beyond the consented purpose.
