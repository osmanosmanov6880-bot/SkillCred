# Pilot playbook: nonprofit / community learning

## Goal
Support voluntary recognition of practical skills obtained through community work without requiring government identity or a paid platform account.

## Workflow
1. Select skills participants can demonstrate with a safe, accessible activity.
2. Offer oral, practical or alternative assessment formats where appropriate.
3. Keep real-world learner identity optional; SkillCred generates an unrelated per-credential subject URN and provide a printed/exported explanation for participants with limited connectivity.
4. Use open-source local verification, document limitations, and avoid exposing participant histories publicly.
5. Collect only aggregate, consented impact metrics and keep small cohorts out of published summaries.

## Stop conditions
Participation requires payment, a public identity profile, intrusive data collection or surrender of a participant's own credential copy.
