# Pilot playbook: university / vocational education

## Goal
Verify that a practical course result can be issued, held by the learner and checked by an independent recipient without creating a central learner profile.

## Participants
One course coordinator, one assessment owner, one privacy contact, 10–30 voluntary learners and at least one external verifier. Do not issue high-stakes credentials during the first rehearsal.

## Workflow
1. Define one observable skill and a rubric before issuing any credential.
2. Issue synthetic records first; then invite informed consent for real pilot records.
3. Keep `subject_id` absent unless the learner explicitly chooses a portable pseudonymous identifier.
4. Give learners a local copy and instructions to export it.
5. Have an external verifier check signatures and a separately fetched status list.
6. Record elapsed verification time, portability result and participant satisfaction using pseudonymous organization codes.
7. Review false accepts/rejects, accessibility and whether non-participation affected course outcomes.

## Stop conditions
Any credential is used to determine grades, licensing or admission before legal, academic and privacy review; a learner cannot withdraw from the research measurement; issuer identity cannot be independently established; or a critical security issue remains open.
