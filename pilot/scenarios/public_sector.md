# Pilot playbook: public sector

## Preconditions
This is a sandbox pilot only. Before use in a government service, complete procurement, accessibility, legal basis, threat modelling, records-retention, incident-response and independent security review. Do not use a pilot credential as the sole basis for benefits, licenses, penalties or access to essential services.

## Workflow
1. Use synthetic records and a non-production environment first.
2. Identify the accountable public authority and the legal status of the issuer.
3. Define the official verification procedure and alternative paper/manual route.
4. Document retention and public-record obligations, including how status-list updates are published.
5. Test service restoration and key rotation with an independent operations team.

## Stop conditions
No lawful basis, lack of an offline/manual alternative, unresolved critical security findings, no public procurement/records approval or a claim that cryptographic validity establishes legal entitlement.
