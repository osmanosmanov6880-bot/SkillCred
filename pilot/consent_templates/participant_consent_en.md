# SkillCred pilot participant consent

**Purpose.** Evaluate the usability of issuing, transferring and verifying practical-skill credentials. The pilot evaluates the technology and process, not a participant's personal worth.

**Voluntary participation.** Declining or withdrawing consent must not affect grades, employment, benefits, service access or the participant's relationship with the organization. A participant may stop metric collection without losing access to their own credential copy.

**Credential identifiers.** A real-world learner ID is optional. When omitted, SkillCred generates a fresh random `urn:uuid:` subject identifier per credential to satisfy the published Open Badges AchievementSubject schema. It is not derived from personal data or stored centrally, but it is visible to anyone who receives the credential; separate credentials use different values.

**Data.** Metrics are limited to a pseudonymous pilot/organization code, scenario, verification time before/after, optional costs and currency, satisfaction from 1 to 5, portability outcome and consent flag. Do not include names, email, identity document numbers, IP addresses or free-text data.

**Retention and contact.** Before collection begins, the organizer must fill in the responsible contact, retention period, withdrawal procedure, deletion process and complaint contact. Do not begin collection until these are defined.
