# SkillCred pilot data processing agreement template

> Template for legal review, not legal advice. Fill every bracket and obtain a privacy specialist's review before real-person pilot data is processed.

1. **Parties and roles:** controller: [organization]; processor, if applicable: [organization]; privacy contact: [contact].
2. **Purpose and term:** limited study of credential portability and verification; period: [dates]. No secondary use without a separate lawful basis.
3. **Data categories:** pseudonymous organizational metrics; credential content only if separately agreed. No special-category data or direct identifiers in the metrics table.
4. **Instructions:** process only according to documented controller instructions and for the stated purpose.
5. **Security:** encryption at rest/in transit, least privilege, secret rotation, logs without tokens or personal data, encrypted backups.
6. **Subprocessors and location:** [list/region/legal basis]. No new subprocessor without the agreed authorization procedure.
7. **Participant rights:** contact for access, correction, erasure and consent withdrawal; response time: [period].
8. **Incidents:** notification, investigation, evidence preservation and remediation process: [procedure].
9. **Deletion/return:** at the end of [period], except lawful retention; evidence of deletion: [procedure].
10. **Audit and liability:** compliance checks, complaint process and applicable law: [agreed legal terms].

Signatures and date: [complete after legal review].
