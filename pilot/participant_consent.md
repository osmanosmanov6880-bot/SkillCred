# SkillCred Pilot — Participant Information and Consent

**Purpose.** This pilot evaluates whether portable, independently verifiable skill credentials reduce the time or effort needed to present and validate practical skills. It is not an employment or educational decision system.

**What participation involves.** You may receive or inspect a test credential, export it, and report whether a separate tool could verify it. Use synthetic or non-sensitive evidence unless the pilot coordinator has separately approved a lawful data-protection basis.

**Data minimization.** A real-world learner identifier is optional. If none is supplied, SkillCred generates a random per-credential `urn:uuid:` subject identifier required by the Open Badges 3.0 AchievementSubject schema; this is not a real-world identity and is not centrally stored. Do not include national IDs, health data, passwords, private keys, or other sensitive personal information in credentials. Credential data may be shared with anyone to whom you give the file. Public status-list publications may permit correlation in some circumstances.

**Voluntary participation.** Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time without penalty. Declining to participate will not affect employment, education, benefits, or access to unrelated services.

**Risks and limitations.** SkillCred 0.3.0 is an experimental pilot profile, not a certified Open Badges implementation. Cryptographic verification does not prove the issuer's legal identity or the quality of a skill assessment. Do not use pilot results for high-impact decisions.
