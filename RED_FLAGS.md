# Red flags and required responses

| Red flag | Meaning | Required response |
|---|---|---|
| One funding source supplies more than 40% of annual income | High dependency | Publish a diversification plan and a funded response to loss of that source |
| One source supplies more than 60% of annual income | Critical concentration risk | Freeze non-essential expansion until the council reviews the contingency plan and any conditions attached to the funding |
| One related group occupies more than one third of participant-council seats | Risk of agenda capture | Independent review of representation, conflicts and term/appointment rules |
| One actor has a majority or unilateral veto over charter, releases and appeals | Unchecked control | Amend governance; separate authority and provide independent appeal |
| Critical operations depend on one person | Single human point of failure | Cross-train maintainers, document tasks and run a six-month absence exercise |
| Credential export or independent verification fails | Users are locked into the platform | Stop claiming portability, fix compatibility and retest independently |
| Status list signature fails or list is stale | Revocation result is unreliable | Report status as unknown; do not silently accept as active; publish corrected list and freshness metadata |
| Security report is ignored | Vulnerability governance failure | Escalate to independent review and publish a redacted disposition |
| Legal criticism or good-faith dissent is punished | Legitimacy failure | Stop retaliation, investigate independently and provide remedy/appeal |
| Growth is celebrated despite no verified public benefit | Vanity metrics replacing value | Pause growth work and repeat product/problem discovery |
| External changes are ignored without reasons | Community process is performative | Publish reasoned dispositions and permit procedural appeal |
| A major security flaw is hidden to protect reputation | Safety and trust failure | Contain, notify affected parties as appropriate, fix and disclose when safe |

Thresholds of 40%, 60% and one third are proposed alert rules, not universal empirical cutoffs. Funding concentration is not proof of wrongdoing; it is a reason to test dependency and prevent financial leverage from becoming unreviewable governance power.

## Mandatory stress tests

### 1. Founder absent for six months

Pass criteria: independent maintainers can issue releases under documented permissions, respond to complaints, restore data and operate status publication without access to the founder's personal accounts or devices.

### 2. Largest funding source disappears

Pass criteria: essential security and maintenance duties continue for a documented period; the funder does not gain a unilateral veto or access to private learner data as a condition of support.

### 3. A community disagrees with a decision

Pass criteria: participants can publish a dissent, appeal the process, propose a compatible alternative and leave with their own data without retaliation for disagreement.

Each failed stress test pauses scaling until an owner, remediation, due date and retest result are recorded.
