# Code of Conduct

SkillCred aims to be a respectful, inclusive, evidence-driven community.

## Expected behavior
- Be considerate, constructive, and precise.
- Critique ideas and implementations rather than attacking people.
- Respect privacy; never publish credentials, personal data, secrets or uncoordinated vulnerability details.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and relevant organizational affiliations.
- Accept that decisions can be challenged through documented processes.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, retaliation against good-faith criticism, deliberate exposure of private data, and attempts to suppress legitimate dissent are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement
Report concerns privately to the maintainers or the governance body's designated independent contact. A reviewer with a conflict of interest must recuse themselves. Reports should be handled confidentially to the extent practical, with proportionate measures, an opportunity to respond, and an appeal path. Retaliation for good-faith reporting is prohibited.

This policy governs community participation; it does not replace applicable law or the project's security disclosure process.
