# SkillCred Charter Amendments Procedure

This is a proposed internal procedure for the SkillCred project. It does not claim that the project is a legally constituted association or foundation.

## Who may propose

Any participant may propose an amendment by opening a public issue or pull request against `CHARTER.md`. The proposal must include the exact text, rationale, affected rights, transition plan, and a section titled **Risks to minority rights and independent implementations**.

## Process

1. **Publication:** proposal remains open for at least 30 calendar days. Emergencies may justify temporary operational controls, but not an undisclosed permanent reduction of rights.
2. **Review:** the governance group checks consistency with applicable law, security, privacy, accessibility, and the non-concentration principles. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed.
3. **Public response:** maintainers publish a response to substantive objections. Dissenting opinions may be attached to the final record.
4. **Decision:** routine clarifications require a two-thirds vote of the governance council. Changes affecting participant rights, exit, independent implementations, amendment procedure, or term limits require a three-quarters vote plus approval by a majority of the independent participant representatives.
5. **No unilateral veto:** the founder, a sponsor, or any single organization cannot approve or block a fundamental amendment alone.
6. **Appeal:** a procedural appeal may be filed within 14 days to the independent review group. Appeals examine process and rights, not merely disagreement with the result.
7. **Publication:** accepted amendments are versioned, dated, summarized in `CHANGELOG.md`, and remain accessible alongside prior versions.
8. **Effective date:** unless the amendment says otherwise, it takes effect at least 14 days after adoption to allow compatible implementations to update.

## Emergency changes

Emergency changes must be narrow, time-limited, documented, and reviewed within 14 days. They cannot silently remove appeal rights, extend an office-holder's term, disable export, or forbid a compatible fork.

## Review and sunset

The procedure itself is reviewed annually. Amendments introducing a new authority or data collection must include an expiration/review date and objective success criteria.
